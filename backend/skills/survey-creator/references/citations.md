# Citations and references

## In the text

Number sources in order of first citation: `[1]`, then `[2]`, and so on. Cite
several at once as `[2, 5]` or a range as `[3-6]`. Put the citation before the
full stop: "... improves recall [4]."

Every number used in the text appears once under References, and every
reference is cited at least once.

## Reference entries

Use the style that matches the conversation: GB/T 7714-2015 for Chinese
surveys, an APA-like style otherwise. Always end the entry with the URL you
found, and mark anything you could not confirm with `[unverified]`.

### APA-like (English)

```
[1] Lewis, P., Perez, E., Piktus, A., et al. (2020). Retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge-intensive NLP tasks. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 33. https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401
[2] Example Organisation. (2024). Title of the technical report. https://example.org/report
[3] Zhang, Y. (2023). Title of the preprint [unverified: venue]. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000
```

### GB/T 7714-2015 (Chinese)

Document type codes: `[J]` journal article, `[C]` conference paper, `[M]`
book, `[D]` thesis, `[R]` report, `[EB/OL]` online resource, `[A]` article in
a collection. For online resources add the access date in square brackets.

```
[1] LEWIS P, PEREZ E, PIKTUS A, et al. Retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge-intensive NLP tasks[C]//Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems. 2020, 33. https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401.
[2] 张三, 李四. 论文题目[J]. 期刊名称, 2023, 45(6): 1-12. https://example.cn/paper.
[3] 示例机构. 报告题目[R/OL]. (2024-05-01)[2026-11-21]. https://example.org/report.
```

GB/T 7714 lists up to three authors, then `et al.` (English) or `等`
(Chinese). Western names are written surname first in capitals with initials.

## What counts as a source

Prefer, in order: peer-reviewed papers, preprints from known groups, technical
reports and official documentation, then reputable reviews or news. Blogs and
vendor pages may be cited for facts about products, never for research claims.
