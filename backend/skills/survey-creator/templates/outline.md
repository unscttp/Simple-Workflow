# Survey outline

Use these sections, in this order. Headings may be translated into the
language of the conversation; keep the numbering. Lengths are guides for a
survey of roughly 3,000-6,000 words.

```markdown
# <Title: "A Survey of <topic>" or "<Topic>: A Review">

## Abstract
150-250 words: the topic, why it matters now, scope, how sources were
collected, the taxonomy in one sentence, the main findings and open problems.

## 1. Introduction
- Context and motivation: why this topic, why now.
- Research questions (RQ1-RQ4), stated exactly.
- Scope: time window, what is included and excluded.
- Contributions of this survey, as a short list.
- How the rest of the survey is organised.

## 2. Background and Definitions
Key terms, the problem formulation and, if useful, a short history. Define
every term the taxonomy uses.

## 3. Method
How sources were found: search queries, sources searched, date of the search,
inclusion and exclusion criteria, how many results were screened and kept.

## 4. Taxonomy of Approaches
One subsection per category (4.1, 4.2, ...). For each: the core idea, the
representative works with citations, strengths and weaknesses. Start the
section with a short overview of the categories and the dimension that
separates them.

## 5. Comparison
A Markdown table of the main works along 4-6 dimensions (see
references/structure.md), followed by what the table shows: trade-offs,
clusters, and where evidence is thin or conflicting.

## 6. Applications and Evaluation
Where the approaches are used, and the datasets, benchmarks and metrics used
to evaluate them, with reported results only as the sources state them.

## 7. Open Challenges
Problems the literature has not solved, each tied to evidence from the
sources (what fails, what is untested, what disagrees).

## 8. Future Directions
Concrete research directions that follow from the challenges.

## 9. Conclusion
Answer each research question in one or two sentences.

## References
[1] ...
[2] ...
```

A survey answers its research questions. Before writing, check that every RQ
is answered by at least one section and that the Conclusion answers each one.
